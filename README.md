## 🌟 Overview

Weight Tracker Pro is a modern, user-friendly web application designed to help you monitor and achieve your weight management goals. Whether you're looking to lose weight, gain muscle, or maintain a healthy lifestyle, our intuitive dashboard and powerful analytics provide the insights you need to stay motivated and on track.

## ✨ Features

- **Intuitive Dashboard**: At-a-glance view of your progress with responsive charts and statistics
- **Goal Setting**: Set personalized weight goals with target dates
- **Detailed Analytics**: Track trends with BMI calculations, weight change rates, and projection insights
- **Progress Photos**: Visual timeline to celebrate your transformation
- **Customizable Reminders**: Never miss a weigh-in with personalized notifications
- **Data Privacy**: Your information is securely stored and never shared
- **Export Functionality**: Download your data in CSV or PDF formats
- **Responsive Design**: Perfect experience on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
